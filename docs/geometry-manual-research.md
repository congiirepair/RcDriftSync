# Geometry Manual Research

Last researched: 2026-05-15

Purpose: collect source-backed references for RC Drift Sync geometry hotspot maps. Use these sources to create app-owned simplified traces and clickable hotspot coordinates. Do not copy copyrighted manual artwork directly into the app unless permission/licensing is confirmed.

## Mapping Priority

1. Shock towers: front and rear upper damper holes.
2. Rear hub carriers: upper link, lower traction, axle-height, and lower tab holes.
3. Front knuckles / steering blocks: top, steering rod, lower, KPI, and spacer locations.
4. Bellcranks / steering racks / slide racks / DDSS: steering link and Ackerman holes.
5. Lower arms: damper hole positions and side/orientation where applicable.
6. Suspension mounts / toe blocks: insert orientation, toe/anti-squat block choice, and spacer locations.

## Confidence Labels

- `official-manual verified`: official manufacturer manual/PDF has visible assembly geometry.
- `official-product verified`: official manufacturer product page/photo verifies part identity or visible holes.
- `retailer-photo traced`: reputable retailer photo or mirrored manual is the best available source.
- `user-provided reference needed`: product needs a clear user/manual photo before accurate mapping.
- `unknown / not enough evidence`: source was not strong enough to map.

## Products Needing User-Provided Images Or More Exact Sources

- Overdose GALM / IFS / curved slide steering: official product pages exist, but exact official manual PDF was not found. Current best assembly source is a mirrored ManualsLib copy, so mark any trace as `retailer-photo traced` unless an official Overdose PDF is found.
- MST RMX 2.5: official support page confirms manuals, but direct PDF URLs were not exposed in crawl. Capture manual files from the support page or ask user for PDF/screenshots before final hole tracing.
- Usukani PDSH/PDSL/PDS-MIX/NGE: official download/product pages exist, but direct official manual PDFs were not exposed by the crawl. Use official downloads manually where possible; otherwise ask for manual PDFs or straight-on part photos.
- Rhino Racing DDSS/Shark/RMX conversions: official Hydra page is available, but DDSS manual/source images are mostly retailer-hosted. Need direct Rhino PDF/manual or user-provided straight-on photos for accurate DDSS hole positions.
- Team AD direct steering / steering blocks / conversions: no official manual found in this pass. Use retailer photos only as provisional, or ask for user-provided product images/manual sheets.
- Third-party parts for RDX/Yokomo such as Topline, Scale Reflex, Wrap-Up Next, Yeah Racing, GTRC, etc.: research only after catalog confirms exact model numbers; otherwise ask for user-provided references.
